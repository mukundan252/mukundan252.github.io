Project 01 · Machine Learning · 2026

AI Predictive Maintenance for Industrial Filter Systems

Designed and deployed a full machine-learning pipeline on real-time industrial sensor data to forecast filter degradation before failure — shifting operations from costly scheduled servicing to intelligent condition-based maintenance.

  • Four sensor streams: Pressure (PT-101), Flow Rate (FT-102), Temperature (TT-103), Differential Pressure (PdT-100)
  • Feature engineering: rolling averages, rate-of-change gradients, anomaly flag indicators
  • Three ML models benchmarked: Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVM — evaluated by failure-class recall
  • Output: real-time health score with confidence band and maintenance trigger signal

Project 02 · Catalysis · Final Year · 2023–2024

Steam Splitting: H₂O → H₂ + O₂

Investigated Nickel-Alumina catalysed thermal decomposition of steam into hydrogen and oxygen, then simulated the downstream use of hydrogen as a combustion enhancer in diesel engines — a pathway towards cleaner transport fuels.

  • Reaction: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (endothermic, ΔH > 0) at 450°C and 1 atm
  • Catalyst: Ni/Al₂O₃ — selected for thermal stability, selectivity and minimal coking
  • ASPEN Plus flowsheet: reactor, heat exchangers, gas separation and recycle loops
  • Hydrogen supplementation of diesel: projected 8–12% NOx reduction in engine exhaust
  • Mass and energy balances validated across all unit operations

Project 03 · Bioprocess · Sustainability · 2023

Bio-Hydrogen from Food Waste via Dark Fermentation

Produced renewable hydrogen through anaerobic dark fermentation of organic food waste using sedimented mycoflora soil from the Pichavaram mangrove forest as a free, naturally-adapted inoculum — a fully circular waste-to-energy approach.

  • Inoculum: indigenous mycoflora from Pichavaram mangrove sediment, naturally adapted to anaerobic conditions
  • Substrate: carbohydrate-rich food waste — zero-cost circular feedstock
  • Batch bioreactor at optimised pH and temperature with microbial community characterisation
  • Measurable H₂ yield confirmed; community analysis identified dominant fermenters
  • Presented at two national conferences as lead author — NIT Warangal and IIChE Chennai 2023

§ 06 · Research

Published research. Real science.

IIChE National Conference · Vel Tech University, Chennai · 2023

Bio-Hydrogen Production from Food Waste Using Sedimented Mycoflora Soil from Pichavaram Mangrove Forest

First national presentation. Demonstrated that indigenously-sourced mycoflora from the Pichavaram mangrove sediment can function as a highly effective, zero-cost inoculum for dark fermentative hydrogen production from organic food waste.

  • Inoculum: sedimented soil collected from Pichavaram mangrove ecosystem, Tamil Nadu
  • Substrate: carbohydrate-rich food waste — characterised by high volatile solids content
  • Batch bioreactor experiments at pH 6.8 and 37°C mesophilic conditions
  • H₂ yield quantified and microbial community characterised via microscopy

National Conference · NIT Warangal · 2023

Mycoflora-Based Anaerobic Fermentation for Renewable Energy: A Waste-to-Hydrogen Approach

Second national paper. Characterised the microbial community from Pichavaram mangrove sediment and demonstrated its efficacy as a biocatalyst for renewable hydrogen production via anaerobic batch fermentation.

  • Detailed characterisation of mycoflora community — dominant fermenting genera identified
  • Kinetic analysis of hydrogen production rate vs substrate loading and incubation time
  • Compared dark fermentation pathways: acetate, butyrate and propionate routes
  • Results contribute to the evidence base for mangrove-ecosystem microbiome applications in bioprocessing
